### Outcome

1. D6 keeps a run-scoped settlement monitor (`sawCharging`, `refreshed`) and calls `refreshAll()`
   only once after the run detail leaves `CHARGING`. A slow period response can no longer restart
   the refresh loop.
2. The normal exports entry calls `GET /exports` without a kind filter, so an `ACCOUNTING` job is
   recoverable after close/reopen and after the provider is remounted (the production reload
   equivalent). A just-queued job and `EXPORT_LIMIT` still open the drawer with `kind=ACCOUNTING`.
3. The E8 real-core scenario creates an approved one-month inactivity period for a separate,
   standalone monthly SEPA member. It freezes that period's fee independently of the simulation,
   then requires the exact amount/currency in the simulation, generated invoice, `/me/invoices`,
   `/rebuts` and D10. The late-month run (`today=2026-10-28`, period 2026-12) passed 6/6.
4. E8-T13 is not present in the current or staged contract. Staged snapshot `1c14122` has none of
   `SavedView.system`, `DashboardCounters.pendingInactivityRequests` or
   `pendingLeaveRequests`; per step 4's explicit fallback, both existing overlays remain unchanged.
