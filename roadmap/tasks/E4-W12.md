6. **D7 (E4-W05 deviations 6 and 7, and E4-W05 review #1).**
   - The list always requests `title` next to `id`, whatever columns are visible, so every row's label reads «Obre {title}». Vitest with a view that hides the «Activitat» column.
   - The registrants table fits 1280 px with long e-mails: the e-mail cell truncates and keeps its full value accessible (title or tooltip), and the header and «Excel · PDF» stay visible. Capture it at 1280 on the real core.
   - The maintenance block hides its «URL» line when `publicUrl` is `null`. Vitest.
7. **03 and the day-grid cells (E4-W05 deviations 9 and 10).**
   - A date in another month shows the month, for example «Dissabte 17 d'octubre · 18:30–20:30 · Central». Vitest.
   - «Ocupada · manteniment» and «Bloq.» never break inside a word in the 375 px cells of 10 and 23. They wrap between words or end with an ellipsis. Vitest on the class names, and a capture.
8. **D4: an activity on every ring (E4-W05 deviation 4).** When an activity blocks every ring of a band, D4 shows one cell «Activitat · {title} · totes les pistes», not one cell per ring. Vitest.
9. **The flaky T-01-26 e2e.** CI was red at `0289e58`, a commit that changed only docs: `e2e/onboarding.spec.ts:37` › «pending account completes the screen once and enters the app» failed with `page.waitForURL: net::ERR_ABORTED; maybe frame was detached?`. ### Assumptions
1. **Step 3:** a plan that has both a current price and a `priceLabel` shows the price. S05 R-05-19 and the S05 field text say that the label replaces a *missing* price. The Cànic's Teràpia has no price, so both readings agree for it.
