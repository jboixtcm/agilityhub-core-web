## Steps
1. **D2: signup notes have no attachments (E3-W09 review #2).** Remove the chip beside «Notes als instructors» on D2: it counts the dog's document files. Fix the T-04-33 test that asserts «3 adjunts». Vitest: a dog with 3 documents and empty notes shows «—» and no chip.
2. **D2: the identity document's label (E3-W09 screens, D2 `NOT_FOUND_PENDING` (b)).** The row names the document's type: «DNI/NIE» for a DNI or NIE, «Passaport» for a passport, in ca, es and en. Vitest.
3. **17: the plan card's price slot (E3-W09 screens 17 (b), R-04-09).** A plan with a `priceLabel` shows it in the price slot: the Cànic's Teràpia reads «condicions i cost segons cada cas», as mockup 17 does. Without a `priceLabel`, the slot shows the price. `conditions` stays in the card's body, as on the other cards. Vitest with the Cànic's Teràpia.
4. **17 in add-dog mode (E3-W09 screens, 17 add-dog; T-04-32).** The add-dog keeps the member's plan, so the modality block is one read-only line with that plan's name and price: no cards and no selection. D2 can still change the plan. Vitest.
5. **D5's default view (E4-W05 deviation 11, S03 §2 D5).** The default view has no plan filter: `planId:eq:plan-member` is a mock fixture id, so a fresh `/abonats` on the real core lists nobody. Only the status chip «Alta» applies by default. Vitest with plans whose ids differ from the fixture's. Extend the real-core e2e so it reads D5 without clearing the filters first.
6. **D7 (E4-W05 deviations 6 and 7, and E4-W05 review #1).**
   - The list always requests `title` next to `id`, whatever columns are visible, so every row's label reads «Obre {title}». Vitest with a view that hides the «Activitat» column.
   - The registrants table fits 1280 px with long e-mails: the e-mail cell truncates and keeps its full value accessible (title or tooltip), and the header and «Excel · PDF» stay visible. Capture it at 1280 on the real core.
   - The maintenance block hides its «URL» line when `publicUrl` is `null`. Vitest.
7. **03 and the day-grid cells (E4-W05 deviations 9 and 10).**
   - A date in another month shows the month, for example «Dissabte 17 d'octubre · 18:30–20:30 · Central». Vitest.
   - «Ocupada · manteniment» and «Bloq.» never break inside a word in the 375 px cells of 10 and 23. They wrap between words or end with an ellipsis. Vitest on the class names, and a capture.
8. **D4: an activity on every ring (E4-W05 deviation 4).** When an activity blocks every ring of a band, D4 shows one cell «Activitat · {title} · totes les pistes», not one cell per ring. Vitest.
9. **The flaky T-01-26 e2e.** CI was red at `0289e58`, a commit that changed only docs: `e2e/onboarding.spec.ts:37` › «pending account completes the screen once and enters the app» failed with `page.waitForURL: net::ERR_ABORTED; maybe frame was detached?`. Wait for the destination's content, not for the navigation event. Prove it with `--repeat-each=20` on that test.
