## Steps
0. **Adopt the staged snapshot (api `f921ff4` or later, E5-T22).** The api's changelog entry of 26-09 lists these changes:
   - `GET /signup`: `plans[].priceLabel` and, in add-dog mode, `plans[].current`;
   - nine list item schemas now require only their row id, because `fields` may leave out every other key: `WeekListItem`, `BookingListItem`, `TrainingBookingListItem`, `DogListItem`, `MemberListItem`, `AttendanceListItem`, `FollowupItem`, `AuditEntryListItem`, and `JobRunListItem` (row id `runId`);
   - renamed items:
     - `GET /class-sessions` answers `ClassSessionListItem`;
     - `GET /ring-blocks` answers `RingBlockListItem`, with no more `anyOf`;
     - an instructor's `GET /members` answers `MemberInstructorListItem`;
     - `GET /class-sessions/{id}/bookings` answers `ClassBookingItem`;
   - new `x-fields` on `/bookings` and `/jobs/{name}/runs`, and `id` in the `x-filterable` of D7's lists;
   - the audit lists read `origin: BACKOFFICE` for an instructor's action.

   Every screen that reads those lists either asks with `fields` for each key it shows, or asks for whole items. An absent key is never read as a value. Prune the `SignupPlan.priceLabel` overlay from `pending.json`.
