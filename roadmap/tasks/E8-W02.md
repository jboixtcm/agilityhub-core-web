# E8-W02 · Member views of billing (12/rebuts, 13, checkout return), screens 14 and 15, and the D10 billing block

## Objective
Work packages S12 **WP-12-G** and S13 **WP-13-D**. The member sees her own receipts with their frozen lines and downloads the PDF, is told when her card could not be charged and fixes it in two taps, sees the real pack balance of each dog on 13, and requests an inactivity period (14) or her leave (15) with every rule of S13 §2 rendered from the api context.
