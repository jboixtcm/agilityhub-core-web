# E5-W02 · Screens 08 and 24 + the D12 ring card — free training (S09 front)

## Objective
Work packages S09 WP-09-D and WP-09-E: a member whose dog may train alone opens the «Entrenaments» tab, reads the computed grid of half-hour slots per ring for today and the next three days, books one (explicitly or through «Qualsevol»), sees the weekly counter of three counted **by session date**, and cancels up to two hours before; an instructor reserves or blocks a ring with no student attached from screen 24 and from the D12 card, with the reason chips and the conflict feedback. Every state comes from the api (`GET /training-slots`, `GET /me/training-summary`): the front never computes a slot, a window, a week or an eligibility. Mocks-first on the S09 schemas of api E5-T01; pixel-faithful to the V8 mockups at 375 px and to D12 at 1280 px.
