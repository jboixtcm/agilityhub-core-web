### Assumptions

- **Snapshot adoption in this task** (as E8-W03/E9-W02 did): the published request fields are only in the staged `2c63db3c`.
- **«Dibuixa'l» (editor)** is not offered: no course editor exists in course-ui or in the spec's WP-16-B′ list; the menu has the other three ways in (no dead option). The empty-list text keeps the spec's literal («…o dibuixa'n un»).
- **Build sheet storage**: `GET/POST /placements/{id}/build-sheet` answer `501 NOT_IMPLEMENTED` in the published contract, so the sheet is generated and downloaded in the browser and not uploaded. The mocks answer 501 like the api.
- **PDFs** use course-ui's own minimal writer (Helvetica, WinAnsi, grey shapes) to avoid a new dependency; print colours are grey levels, not theme colours. The marker sheet prints the AprilTag 36h11 fiducial (the planner's and AR app's family, §14.1) rather than a QR code, at the stored physical size including the quiet zone, capped to 190 mm on A4 (then the printed size is stated); a marker without `aprilTagId` uses the planner's `aprilTagIdFromMarkerUid`.
- **New geometry default**: six canonical markers A–F (the AR export requires A–F; §14 prevails over §2's «4 per defecte»); new markers get the planner's uid and a free AprilTag id. No-go zones are edited as rectangles (stored as 4-point polygons); free polygons are not drawn in this cut.
- **«Substituït» in D16's history**: the api stores no reason; a DISMANTLED setup whose `dismantledAt` equals another setup's `builtAt` was replaced by it (R-16-07: one transaction). If the real api's timestamps differ, it reads «Desmuntat».
- **Thumbnails**: a PNG drawn on an `OffscreenCanvas` with the club's course tokens at save time (best effort; no thumbnail → placeholder). Mock fixture images are white-paper SVGs built with a colour matrix (no literal colour).
- `warningThresholdM` is sent as `null` (the club's `courses.defaultWarningThresholdM`, which an INSTRUCTOR cannot read); course-core's rules take their thresholds from the ring and doors.
- Metadata edits cover name (default locale, other locales kept), discipline, designer, AgilityHub level, tags, notes; club `levelIds` are shown but not edited here (needs `levels.enabled`, ADMIN-only parameter).
- **Bundles**: Vite 8 (rolldown) moves the modules shared by the two lazy chunks and the entry into a `list-helpers-*.js` chunk loaded at start, so «clubs-admin initial» now counts `index`, `list-helpers` and `jsx-runtime` (424.68 kB, as before); the course chunks got their own entries (ruling E91's pattern) and are excluded from «clubs-admin all»; «clubs all» rose 420 → 470 kB (new measure 428.07 + ~10 %) because of the three lazily-loaded `admin-courses` locale chunks every app carries.
