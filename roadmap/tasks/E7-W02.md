# E7-W02 · Screens 11, 12 and 30 + push (S11 WP-11-E)

## Objective
Work package S11 WP-11-E: the member reads her notification feed on screen 11 with the icon, colour, relative time, the «i per SMS» mark and the native action buttons the code fixes ([CANVIA DE CLASSE], [AGAFA LA PLAÇA]); entering the screen marks everything read and the bell of screen 03 goes quiet; the static «Avisos» card of screen 12 becomes live (email per category, the fixed «+SMS», the class-reminder select, the push toggle) and the «Idioma» selector keeps writing `Account.locale`; the app subscribes to web push in context, with a service worker that shows the notification and opens its deep link; and screen 30 «Info» — already built by E2-W09 with its FAQ · Normes · pages tabs — gets the FAQ behaviour S11 requires.
