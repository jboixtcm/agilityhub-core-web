### Assumptions
- **A1. One saver for both screens.** The review asked for the same rule on D10 and 12. One implementation in `packages/api-client` replaces the two copies, so the rule cannot drift again. Both screens keep their own outbox key and scope: account and club on 12, member on D10.
- **A2. Open page versus departure.** While the page is open, every answer is shown: a failure or refusal puts back the api's value and says why. So whatever the visit took over from the outbox goes once nothing is unsaved. Only a departure waits for the 2xx of its final `PUT`.
