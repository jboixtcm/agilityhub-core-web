# E11-W07 · The web adopts the api's E11-T15 contract

## Objective
E11-T15 (api, verified round 1 on `372b1255`, snapshot staged as `openapi.next.json` at `a7c85d50`) changed the money chain: a monthly member without `nextInvoiceDate` is a simulation incident (`NO_NEXT_INVOICE_DATE`), the plan change to a monthly plan accepts a `nextInvoiceDate`, a new `PUT /api/v1/members/{id}/next-invoice-date {nextInvoiceDate, reason}` sets or corrects it (ADMIN, ±12 months, audited), the remittance carries `requestedCollectionDate` and `submissionDue`, a run past its collection date cannot be rolled back (`COLLECTION_DATE_PASSED`), and a manual receipt takes a `period`. The organizer's verification of E11-T15 (21-10) also found that D10's reactivation drawer pre-fills the LEFT member's stale date — the api now refuses a date before the club's current month (E11-T15 round 2, N1) and the web must propose the right one. This task adopts all of it in the back office. The round-2 contract of E11-T15/E11-T13 and E11-T19's console entry come later (E11-W08).

**Each change comes with a test that fails before it** (MSW handlers mirror the contract; a mock-browser test per screen).
