3. **Complete e2e.** `pnpm e2e:docker`.
4. **Workspace checks.** `pnpm turbo run lint typecheck test build` and `pnpm i18n:check`.
5. **Screenshots against the real core**, at 375 px (PWA) and 1280 px (back office):
   - 16, 17, 18, 19 and «enviada»;
   - 19 for a monthly plan on a day before 16 and on a day from 16 on, and for Pack 6;
   - 03 after the welcome link;
   - add-dog 17/19, and 13 with a pending dog;
   - D1 before and after the validation;
   - D2: normal, add-dog, readmission, and a `NOT_FOUND_PENDING` claim;

   Write `roadmap/evidence/E3-W09/screens.md` as in E3-W05: one row per screen, its mockup, and every visible difference, marked **accepted** (with its decision id) or **new**.
   - Check every description against the **final** PNG before writing it (counts, values, texts).
   - **accepted** needs a decision that authorizes the deviation. A defect with a fix task already scheduled is **new**, with the task id.
   - (These two rules come from the Codex review of E3-W05.)
6. **Traceability.** Write `roadmap/evidence/E3-W09/trace-tests.md` as in E3-W05, with two rules from the Codex review of E3-W05:
   - one row per test case, with its fully qualified name, its location and its result; no collapsed rows;
   - an id is «covered» only when a test asserts what its §11 row says (for example, T-04-33 changes the plan and asserts the `dryRun` recalculation; T-04-29 receives an api `400` with `fieldErrors`). Otherwise it is «partial» or «untested», with the reason. Add a section «Audit tests»: for each web item of the consolidated report's required-tests list, the test that proves it and its result:
   - a NIE and a passport submission (Vitest and real-core e2e);
   - a retry after a lost 201;
   - the total on 19, and the labels on days 5 and 17;
   - a pack-plan quote;
   - a dog edit after an add-dog;
   - 422 codes on the D2 fields;
   - D1 fresh right after a validation, with no wait in the e2e.
7. **Gate lines.** For each line of «Gate E3 (front)» in `roadmap/ROADMAP.md`, give one line of evidence from this run.
