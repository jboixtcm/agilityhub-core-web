### Assumptions

- No `packages/api-client/openapi/openapi.next.json` was staged, so the S04 frontend subset remains in `pending.json` and generated types were rebuilt from the merged snapshots.
- The task says “3 plans” while approved screen 17 and the S04 seed show Abonat, Pack 6, Pack 10, and Teràpia. The fixture follows the approved screen and exposes all four.
- S04 defines no `payment` field on `MemberDogSignupRequest`; the current payment method is therefore read-only and omitted from the add-dog submission.
- The member signup response has no `memberId`/`signupToken` checkout context. The add-dog fixture returns `checkout.required=false`; public signup covers the required Stripe redirect.
