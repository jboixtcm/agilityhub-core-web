5. Impersonation banner in `apps/clubs` when `me.impersonation` is present («Estàs veient l'app com {member}» + «Surt»), and handoff entry point «Obre el backoffice» for accounts with ADMIN/INSTRUCTOR profiles (`POST /auth/handoff` → open `clubsadmin` host with the code).
