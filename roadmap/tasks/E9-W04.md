### Assumptions

- **No «verificat» status** in the contract (`BuildObstacle.status`: NOT_PLACED, CURRENT, PLACED, SKIPPED, NEEDS_CHECK): the tap's third state is `NEEDS_CHECK` («per revisar»).
- **«Cap» source** is offered only as the kind «Buida»: the api refuses any other kind without a source (`SETUP_SOURCE_REQUIRED`).
- **08's chip keeps selecting the ring** (booking needs it); the viewer opens from the line under the chips.
- **D12** has day columns: the «capçalera de pista» is a strip of the rings' setups above the grid.
- **«per {instructor}»** is not shown: `RingSetup` carries only `builtByAccountId` and no instructor reader maps it to a name.
- **Build sheet on the phone**: only the stored one (`GET /placements/{id}/build-sheet`) when the placement has it; generating it needs the admin sheet texts (not shipped to the members' app).
- **QR join**: the six-character code only; no QR is drawn (no QR library in the app).
- Mock mode injects no SSE transport (MSW cannot share one world between two pages): the store polls there, which also exercises INC-60's default; two phones are proved only against the real core.
- The real-core stage seeds its own geometry/course/placement/setup through the api (the demo seed has none); the course's warnings are course-core's (critical → `force`, admin).
