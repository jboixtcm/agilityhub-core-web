### Outcome

1. **A38, the members' app router.** `apps/clubs` now changes screens with the History API: `apps/clubs/src/router.ts` has `navigate(path, {replace, state})` (push or replace, then a `popstate` the app already listens to) and `useInAppLinks()`, a document-level click handler that takes a plain left click on a same-origin link to a screen. It leaves to the browser any modifier or middle click, `target`, `download`, `data-reload`, another origin, `mailto:`/`tel:`, `/api/`, `/oauth2/`, `/connect/`, `/.well-known/`, files and same-page anchors. The app's own `navigate` calls (booking, 12/14/15, activation, access, the legacy redirects) go through it, and the shell scrolls the new screen to the top. A tab no longer renews the token nor re-reads `/branding` and `/me`, and Back returns. The signup flow (16–19) keeps its full loads (`loadSignupStep`): it was built and tested around them, and its public shell has no session to keep. The shell gives each route its own `Suspense` boundary (keyed by the route's pattern): a screen the user left unmounts at once, as when the screens were static, instead of staying mounted and hidden while the next screen's chunk loads, where a late answer could still act. E9-W05's R-16-11 test (leaving a build session while FINALITZA runs) found it after the rebase onto `3ed54b4` (log 47). Under that boundary a shown screen must never suspend again, or the boundary hides it under the loading state until the piece is ready: the screens loader also loads the app's namespaces not loaded yet (with the chunk, in parallel), and the course blocks other screens embed (07's lines, 08's, 24's link) and `useStartBuildSession` wait for `courses` without suspending. PR #7's first CI found it: E8-W02's checkout return on 07 lost its «Estem confirmant el pagament…», hidden while 07's course lines suspended; a probe found 04, 25 and the receipts hidden again the same way, 70 ms after showing, for 300 ms (log 56).
