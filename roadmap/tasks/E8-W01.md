# E8-W01 · D6 «Facturació» + remittances + accounting export

## Objective
Work packages S12 **WP-12-F** and the front half of **WP-12-H**. The administrator opens `/facturacio?mes=YYYY-MM`, simulates the month and reads the incidents first, sees the cash members with their planned leave date and the four (or five) KPIs, generates the month behind a strong confirmation, charges the cards when the club has Stripe, rolls the whole generation back by typing «RETROCEDIR», filters the receipts with the chips of the mockup, marks a selection paid, opens a receipt drawer and runs every state action on it, issues a manual adjustment receipt, downloads the accounting export, and manages the remittance files on `/facturacio/remeses`. Everything is decided by the api: the front never recomputes a total, a number, a status or the rollbackability of a run. Mocks-first on the S12 contract of api E8-T01; pixel-faithful to the V7 mockup at 1280 px.

### Assumptions
- **A1 (mockup data):** the fixture keeps the mockup's figures as the task lists them, and stays consistent where that is possible: September has 168 receipts adding up to 6.480 € (42 «Abonat» fees of 60 €, 118 maintenance fees of 30 €, Laura 90 €, two inactivity fees, four cash receipts), 162 COLLECTING, 4 PENDING, 2 FAILED. Three mockup inconsistencies are kept as drawn: «Joan Vila» is an incident *and* a cash member with a receipt (the incident is another member with the same name, its own id); the cash card lists 2 members while the KPI counts 4 cash receipts; Pere Soler's «2026-0871 · Agost 2026» row is August's (`period` 2026-08), so it shows under «Agost 2026», not in September's list (step 6's always-present `period:eq:{period}`). September's two returns are 2026-0972 and Pere Soler's 2026-1039.
- **A2 (collection date):** neither `PeriodRun` nor `SimulationKpis` carries a collection date, so the KPI's «data de cobrament: {dd/mm}» comes from `GET /billing/runs/{id}` (`collectionDate`) once a run exists, and is left out before (proposal Q1).
- **A3 (Concepte):** `InvoiceListItem` has no lines; its `concept` is already «first frozen description (+n)» as the api composes it, so D6 shows `concept` verbatim (never rebuilt).
- **A4 («Import de la remesa»):** the KPI shows `simulation.kpis.total` (6.480 €, the mockup's figure), cash included; the remittance's own total (6.240 €) is on the remittances page.
- **A5 (confirmation sentence):** «…avançarà al dia {nextDate}.» with `{nextDate}` = the club-format «1 d’octubre» / «1 de novembre», so Catalan elides correctly; the spec writes «al dia 1 de {mes+1}».
- **A6 ([COBRA LES TARGETES]):** offered on `GENERATED` only, as the snapshot documents (`409 INVALID_STATE` on any other state); step 5 says GENERATED or CHARGING. While CHARGING the page shows «Cobrant les targetes…» and polls.
- **A7 (period filter):** the month is the header's and is always sent as `period:eq:{mes}`; it is not one of the list's editable filters (a `filter=period:…` link sets the month instead). The status chip stays when the month changes.
- **A8 (manual receipt):** the description limit is the contract's 140 (`ManualInvoiceLine.description.maxLength`), not step 6's 120; `taxPercent` defaults to 0 (B3); the member search is `GET /members?q=` (active members), as D5's.
- **A9 (PDF):** a blank tab is opened on the click (no pop-up blocker), then pointed at the PDF blob; without a tab the file is saved with the api's `Content-Disposition` name.
- **A10 (selection):** only the receipts «Marca cobrat» applies to (PENDING/FAILED, not CARD; E87) can be selected; a selected receipt the list later reads in another state leaves the selection.
- **A11 (receipts list export):** the universal list keeps its «Excel · PDF» (`GET /invoices/export`, already published) next to «Exporta per a comptabilitat».
- **A12 (COLLECTING by card):** reads «remesat» as step 6 says, for SEPA and card alike (Q4).
- **A13 (`run.skipped[]`):** step 4 asks to render the run's skipped members «as the same row shape». Every member the simulation lists as an incident is skipped already (the footnote says so), so «Omesos de la generació» lists only skipped members the incidents do not (same `memberId` and `code`); in the fixtures the two lists are equal and the block does not show.
- **A14 (where the extras sit):** [＋ Rebut manual] is a round «+» at the end of the header's month actions (step 6 asks for a discreet entry; the receipts toolbar is full at 1280 px). «Remeses ›» sits in the «Import de la remesa» KPI and only for clubs with `SEPA_XML` (a cash-only club has no remittance: no dead link).
- **A15 (mock world):** the billing world is built on the first billing request, not at import: the browser worker imports every mock world on every page, and only D6 needs this one.
