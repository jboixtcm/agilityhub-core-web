1. **W1 (major) — a deep link does not survive the sign-in.** `packages/auth/src/session.tsx:99-103` (`navigateToAccess` assigns `/entrar` with no return address) and `:170-184` (`RequireAuth` calls `navigate("/entrar")`); after the sign-in the members' app goes to `routeAfterLogin` (`apps/clubs/src/App.tsx:674` password, `:796` reset/magic request, `:1025` the magic link's «Continua» on `/activacio`) and the back office to `landingPath` (`apps/clubs-admin/src/App.tsx:827` handoff, `:905` password). Scenario: a signed-out member (new phone, or the 30-day session expired) taps N-15's «AGAFA LA PLAÇA» (`/espera/:id`) or N-04's link (`/reserves/:id`), or the admin opens N-01's D2 link (`/preinscripcions/:id`) → `/entrar` → after the login or the magic link the app lands on `/inici` (or D1) and the claim, booking or signup has to be found by hand. Rules: S11 R-11-11, S01 §2 01/02. Fix: `RequireAuth`/`navigateToAccess` keep the requested path + search (a `?next=` on `/entrar`, mirrored into `localStorage` under one key so the magic link's new tab finds it, with a 15-minute expiry); after `login`, the magic link's «Continua» and the back office's handoff/login, the app goes there instead of the landing **only when** it is a same-origin app path (starts with a single `/`, no `//`, not `/entrar`, `/activacio`, `/acces`, `/alta…`) **and** `routeAfterLogin` would otherwise give the profile's landing: onboarding (`/benvinguda`) and 03b (`/perfil-acces`) come first and keep the return path for their own «continua»/`selectProfile(MEMBER | INSTRUCTOR)`; an ADMIN handoff to clubs-admin drops it. The path is consumed once. 3. **W3 (major, desktop multi-tab) — three tabs restoring at once sign everyone out.** `packages/auth/src/auth-client.ts:571-591` (`refresh` coalesces within one tab only), `:634-679` (`performRestoreSession` → `refresh()`), `:969-978` (`performRefresh`).
