3. **One error helper (review #8).** A shared helper in `packages/api-client` reads a field error from both `details.fieldErrors[]` and `details.field`.

4. **The closed-days literal (review #7).** `admin-scheduling:templates.bandForm.closedDays` becomes a label, so it reads well with one day or several, and with «dissabte» too:
   - ca «Dies de tancament: {days}. Aquesta plantilla no admet franges.»;
   - es «Días de cierre: {days}. Esta plantilla no admite franjas.»;
   - en «Closed days: {days}. This template cannot have time bands.».
   
   `{days}` is the long weekday names joined as a list in the reader's language, for example «dilluns i dimarts», «lunes y martes», «Monday and Tuesday». Vitest on the ca text with two days.
