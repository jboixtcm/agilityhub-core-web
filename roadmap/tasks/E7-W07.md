## Steps
1. **A late `/branding` answer refreshes the cache (E7-W06 review #2; A4).**
   - At the 4 s limit the boot starts with the cached branding, as now. But the request is no longer aborted: it finishes in the background, and `writeCachedBranding` stores its answer, so the next load starts fresh.
   - The running page keeps what it booted with.
   - Test: a late answer still updates the cache; a late failure leaves the cache as it was.
2. **A failed preference save shows and retries the member's last choice (E7-W06 review #3; Q3, ruling E85: option a).**
   - After the `GET` that follows a failure, put the kept patch back on top of the api's values. It shows as a pending change, with the saver's pending text, and is sent again.
   - The outbox entry goes only when that choice gets a `2xx`, or when the member changes the same preference again (the new choice wins).
   - Test the review's scenario: overlapping `PUT`s, and the last one fails. The `GET` answers X = false, the page shows X = true as pending, and the retry saves it.
   - Test the departure too: the member leaves right after the failure. The next visit within 5 minutes shows the pending choice before it is saved.
3. **`MEMBER_ERASED` on every mutation of an erased member (E7-W06 review #4; A8; S14 §5).**
   - In MSW, every S03 and S13 mutation of the erased member answers `409 MEMBER_ERASED`, not `404`. List those routes in the report.
   - D10 shows that answer as final, with no retry, on each of those actions.
   - The fixture keeps the fields that the published contract requires (INC-53 item 10 asks the api about them). It nulls every other field that R-14-15 nulls.
4. **One submission-key helper for every keyed write (E7-W06 review #5 and Q4; Q1, ruling E85).**
   - `isUnanswered` holds the only rule:
     - these keep the key: a network error, a gateway's answer without the api's body, any `5xx`, and `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED {IN_PROGRESS}`;
     - a `2xx`, or a `4xx` with the api's body, retires it.
   - Every keyed write uses `createSubmissionKeys`:
     - `followup.ts`: drop its local `useSubmissionKeys`, and keep review #7's own-key check;
     - `ring-blocks.ts`, `TrainingPage.tsx` and `ui/src/attendance-sheet.ts`;
     - `ConfirmPage.tsx`: its local 5xx exception goes, since the rule now covers it;
     - D14's row read, with A3's 5 minutes, like the read-all.
   - Q4's writes:
     - 13's activity registration keeps one key per submission;
     - D4c's and D7's publication and cancellation retire the key after a refusal;
     - `POST /seat-holds` and `POST /waitlist-entries` declare no key, so they stay without one (CONVENCIONS_API §7). If a lost answer can leave a second hold or entry, say so in the report as a proposal for the api.
   - Test each write against the rule. Include a `503` with the api's body (the key is kept) and a `422` (the key is retired).
5. **The weekly limit in the mock's registrants (E5-W05 round-3 review #2).**
   - `registrantsOf` skips a dog once it has reached its booking week's limit. R-08-03 gives 2 classes in W0 and 1 in W1, and R-08-19 counts the back office's bookings too.
   - Test that no dog of the default world is booked over its week's limit. Capture `D10-reserves-1280.png` again.
6. **R3-A3 is not accepted (E5-W05 round-3 review #5; ruling E85).**
