## New differences (summary for the gate review)

1. **Placeholders are not resolved on the public form.** 18 shows `{twoDogsMonthlyFee}`; 19 and the add-dog 19 show `{deadlineDay}`. The MSW fixtures hide both.
2. **Screen 19:** no «Total a pagar al club» line (T-04-32), and the `MANUAL` instructions (cash text) appear under «Domiciliació».
3. **Screen 17:** the plan cards repeat the intro paragraphs from the seed descriptions; packs show «Entrada 0,00 €».
4. **D1:** the risk card is unbounded (16 rows); «07:30» vs «7:30»; title style; greeting comma.
5. **D2:** the full DNI is shown (the mockup masks it); danger-filled [REBUTJA]; «mascle» in lower case.
6. **Shell:** no field for the tax id in the footer (`club.taxId` proposal); in-field labels vs visible labels (S4); primary-button contrast 3.32:1 (S6).
7. **Evidence only:** the existing `13-my-dogs-core-375.png` is captured while loading; the loaded capture was added.
