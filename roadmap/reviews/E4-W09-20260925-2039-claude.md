## Findings
1. **minor** `apps/clubs-admin/src/planning/SelectedClassCard.tsx:543` with `packages/api-client/src/mocks/calendar-handlers.ts:696-702`: the card and the mock disagree about an existing class on a closed day.
   - **What happens:** on a closed day the card offers only the class's own time and shows the note, but [ACCEPTA] stays enabled for capacity, ring, levels and instructors (the executor's assumption 3). The mock re-validates the whole class on every PATCH that changes more than notes (`sessionProblem` → `timeProblem`). So every such save on that day gets `422 OUTSIDE_OPENING_HOURS` in mock mode.
   - **Inconsistent with the block drawer:** `RingBlockDrawer.tsx:469`, in edit mode on a closed day, disables [DESA EL BLOQUEIG]. The two D4 editors of an existing item on a closed day therefore behave differently.
   - **Why it matters:** Step 3 asks for "submission blocked", and the task's conventions say "mocks behave like the api". The new card test (`CalendarPage.test.tsx`, «R-02-09 Sunday absent…») checks only the options and the note, never what [ACCEPTA] does.
   - **Suggested fix:** until the api answers question 3, choose one rule and test it. Either:
     - disable [ACCEPTA] for anything but notes on a closed day (matches the mock, the block drawer and S06 §3 "dins `club.openingHours` del dia"); or
     - make the mock skip the opening check when the PATCH does not touch `startTime`/`endTime`.
